How it works

BEA builds the PCE price index from a few hundred detailed spending lines, and most of their prices come straight from the CPI and PPI that BLS publishes two weeks earlier. This nowcast follows the Chicago Fed's Flash PCE approach: it estimates each of the 221 lines BEA publishes from the same CPI or PPI series BEA uses, forecasts the lines BEA prices itself (nonprofits, imputed bank services, foreign travel) from their own history, and combines them with BEA's Fisher formula using last month's actual spending shares.

For every line several versions are tried (seasonally adjusted or not, with or without a correction for BEA's own seasonal adjustment, recent averages), and the one with the smallest error over the previous three years is used. The track record above is out of sample: each month's estimate only uses information from before that month.

The page updates itself on CPI, PPI and PCE release days. Before CPI and PPI are out, lines use their own recent trend and the estimate is marked waiting; once one is out it is provisional; with both in it is final.
